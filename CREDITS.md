# Background credits

Each plate was matted onto a 6144x3456 canvas in its own paper tone rather than
cropped, so the cartouches and captions survive. No AI upscaling was used on the
archival scans — it destroys engraved linework.

## 1-turing-von-neumann.jpg

AI-generated poster, provenance unknown. Upscaled 4x with Real-ESRGAN
(realesrgan-x4plus). Note the logic-gate and state-machine labels are nonsense.

## 2-babbage-analytical-engine.jpg

"The General Plan of Mr Babbage's Great Calculating Engine", 6 August 1840.
Photograph by Arnold Reinhold of the plan held at the Computer History Museum.
Flat-field corrected for the photograph's vignette, white point warmed to cream.

- Licence: **CC BY 4.0** — attribution required if redistributed
- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Babbage_Analytical_Engine_Plan_1840_CHM.agr.jpg

## 3-impressio-librorum.jpg

"Impressio Librorum" (the invention of printing), from *Nova Reperta*, after Jan
van der Straet, engraved c. 1590.

- Licence: **CC0** — Museum Plantin-Moretus
- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:De_uitvinding_van_de_boekdrukkunst,_anoniem,_Museum_Plantin-Moretus,_PK_OPB_0186_005.jpg

## 4-barbette-carriage.jpg

"15 Inch Barbette Carriage, Front Pintle, as designed by Capt. J. J. Rodman",
Watervliet Arsenal, 1863. Off-theme for a computing set, but the closest tonal
match to the palette. Scan-bed white behind the torn edge replaced with the
sheet's own paper tone.

- Licence: **Public domain** — U.S. National Archives and Records Administration
- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:15_inch_Barbette_Carriage,_Front_Pintle,_As_Designed_by_Capt._J._J._Rodman._-_NARA_-_122207770.jpg
